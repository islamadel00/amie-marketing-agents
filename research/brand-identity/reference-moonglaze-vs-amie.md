# Brand reference: moonglaze.co vs amie-sa.com (2026-10-25)
Source: Claude Desktop walkthrough (Chromium, mobile 390×844 + desktop 1440). Full report pasted in chat; key facts kept here.

## moonglaze (reference only — do not copy its exact marks)
- Palette (3 colours): cream `#F8F6EA` background · charcoal `#232323` buttons/dark sections · brown `#774D30` logo/links; secondary text `#848484`; product image bg ≈ `#B4AAA2` (same for every product).
- Type: roc-grotesk — headings 600 large (36px), body 300 light; uppercase labels with letter-spacing.
- Layout: lots of whitespace (~40px section padding), sharp 0px cards, 4 per row desktop / 2 mobile, full-width ADD TO BASKET on card.
- Imagery: uniform studio product shots, heavy macro (texture), UGC video carousel with product tags, shop-the-look hotspots.
- Product page: short; name + price on one line; real-colour shade dots; two buttons; accordions; how-to video; sticky add bar; sticky details on desktop.
- Speed: FCP mobile home ~2.2s.

## a'mie today
- Colours: white `#FFFFFF`, off-white `#F9F8F6`, headings `#5E2E21`, card text `#6B595C`, CTA `#663C3C`, badges `#8B2635`, sale red `#DE0000`/`#F25F5F`, free-shipping orange `#F97316` + green, Tailwind greys → too many colours.
- Type: Readex Pro 400 everywhere, little contrast in size/weight.
- Cards: 23px radius, icons only (no full button), mixed white/grey/lifestyle backgrounds → grid not uniform.
- Strongest asset: macro hero image. No video anywhere.
- Product page: long (tabby, shipping note, share, tabs, long grey cards), related products, sticky add bar.
- Speed: FCP mobile home ~3.6s, load ~41s.
- Copy: Arabic literary + direct; inconsistent hamza (اشتري/يبدا).

## Principles to adopt (in a'mie's own colours/voice)
1. Narrow palette, warm off-white background (own tone, not `#F8F6EA`).
2. One background colour for all product photos.
3. Type contrast: big bold headings + light body (Readex Pro 300 needs custom CSS on Zid).
4. More whitespace, fewer homepage sections.
5. UGC/community video (needs app/code for shoppable).
6. Texture/macro section.
7. Real-colour swatches where variants exist.
8. Second image on hover (theme-dependent).
9. Short product page + accordions.
10. Short, consistent section titles; fix spelling.
Not easily on Zid: sticky details (desktop), hotspots, shoppable video.
