## 1. What naming hierarchies do brands use, and which parts go in title vs. variant selector vs. subtitle?

### Takeaway
Almost every leading DTC body/beauty brand uses a short **branded-house** stack: [Signature/franchise name] + [product-type descriptor] as the title, with **scent / flavor / shade as a variant** (selector or "- variant" suffix), size as a selectable option or spec, and the brand name **omitted** from titles on their own site (retailers add it). Line/collection names mostly live in navigation/collections, not in the title.

### Cited Findings
- General model: a beauty naming system usually has three levels — master brand, product lines/collections, individual SKUs; in a branded-house model the master brand leads and line/product names act as descriptors. A naming-architecture framework decides whether products use master brand + descriptor or standalone names. — [NameStormers: Naming Architecture](https://www.namestormers.com/capabilities/naming-architecture/); [SmashBrand: Product naming conventions](https://www.smashbrand.com/articles/product-naming-conventions/); [RainCastle: Product Naming (PDF)](https://www.raincastle.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/rcc_article_product-naming-and-branding.pdf)

### Inferences
- Dominant DTC formula on own-site: **Title = [Signature name] [Descriptor/format]**; **Variant selector = scent / flavor / shade + size**; **Subtitle/tagline = benefit** ("Skin Tightening Body Cream", "Nourishing Glaze", "Body Luminizer"). Brand name and size are not in the own-site title; retailers add both.
- Two equally valid "axes" for the leading word: (a) **franchise-led** (Sol de Janeiro "Brazilian Bum Bum", Huda "Easy Bake", Summer Fridays "Jet Lag") when the format is the hero; (b) **scent-led** (Tree Hut, Bath & Body Works) when the scent is the hero and formats repeat across scents. A brand with many scents across many formats (like a'mie's scrubs/butters/mists) typically chooses one and applies it everywhere.
- For a'mie on Zid: put scent/shade into Zid product options (variants) wherever products differ only by scent/shade/size, so one product page and one URL accumulates reviews and SEO.

## 2. Signature/fantasy names vs. descriptive product-type words

### Takeaway
Leaders almost never ship a fantasy name alone on the product page: the signature name is paired with a plain descriptor (format, often + function), so the title both builds equity and is searchable. Signature names alone are used in speech/social and in set names once the product is iconic.

### Cited Findings
- Descriptive names make function clear but are weak trademarks; strong marks are fanciful/arbitrary/suggestive; suggestive names are a middle ground; recommended mix: descriptive for core, themed names where personality adds value; fragrance names can be fantasy if notes/descriptor appear underneath. — [SmashBrand](https://www.smashbrand.com/articles/product-naming-conventions/); [cosmetiFULL naming & trademark guide](https://cosmetifull.com/cosmetic-brand-naming/); [Trademark Engine](https://www.trademarkengine.com/blog/can-you-trademark-a-beauty-product-name/); [Segment8](https://blog.segment8.com/posts/product-naming/); [Scentsorie: Art of naming a fragrance](https://scentsorie.com/blogs/stories/product-naming-strategy)
- Pairing examples: "Brazilian Bum Bum **Cream**" (+ subtitle "Skin Tightening Body Cream"); "Body Lava **Body Luminizer**"; "Butta Drop **Whipped Oil Body Cream**"; "Jet Lag **Mask + Moisturizer**"; "Easy Bake **Loose Baking & Setting Powder**"; "Bermuda **Dark Spot Oil**"; "Charlotte's Magic **Cream**". — [SdJ](https://soldejaneiro.com/products/brazilian-bum-bum-cream); [Fenty](https://fentybeauty.com/products/body-lava-body-luminizer-penny-drop); [Summer Fridays](https://summerfridays.com/collections/all-products); [Huda](https://hudabeauty.com/en-us/products/easy-bake-loose-baking-setting-powder-hb00140m); [Bushbalm](https://bushbalm.com/products/dark-spot-treatment); [Grazia on CT gift sets](https://graziadaily.co.uk/beauty-hair/makeup/best-charlotte-tilbury-christmas-gifts/)
- rhode is the counter-example: purely descriptive ingredient+format names ("peptide lip treatment", "glazing milk", "peptide lip tint", "pocket blush") with the **fantasy layer pushed into flavors/shades** (strawberry glaze, salted caramel, jelly bean, teacup). — [rhode](https://www.rhodeskin.com/products/peptide-lip-treatment); [Amazon rhode Glazed Trio](https://www.amazon.com/Rhode-Treatment-Strawberry-Skincare-Collection/dp/B0FY4CZBWY)
- Glossier mixes coined hero names with a category underneath: "Balm Dotcom" (lip balm/skin salve, flavors incl. mint, birthday cake; LE Peppermint Bark, Ginger Snap), "Cloud Paint" (cheek colour; shades Glisten, Afterglow, Crimson), "Boy Brow", fragrance "You". — [Space NK Glossier](https://www.spacenk.com/uk/inside-space/most-wanted/best-glossier-products.html); [Glossier Blueprint/Top Shelf Essentials](https://uk.glossier.com/products/blueprint-set)
- Charlotte Tilbury uses one fantasy franchise ("Pillow Talk") as an umbrella across lipstick, blush, eyeshadow, lip kits, with sub-names ("Original", "Medium", "Naughty Talk"). — [CT Pillow Talk collection](https://www.charlottetilbury.com/us/products/makeup/collections/pillow-talk)

### Inferences
- Rule of thumb from the examples: **one fantasy element per title, max.** Either the hero is fantasy and the variant is descriptive (Body Lava – Penny Drop is an exception, both fantasy), or the product is descriptive and the variant carries the fun (rhode, Tree Hut).
- Reserve fantasy/signature names for the 3–6 hero products that deserve equity (for a'mie e.g. body glow oil, highlighter sticks, signature musk/scent family); keep supporting items (accessories, favors) purely descriptive.
- Arabic store implication (inference): a descriptor in Arabic (e.g. "مقشر"، "زبدة جسم"، "زيت لمعان") should always follow the signature name so Arabic search and AI answers can classify the product.

## 5. Consistency rules: capitalization, brand prefix, size, spelling, repetition

### Takeaway
Pick one casing style and apply it everywhere (rhode = all lowercase; most others = Title Case); omit the brand from own-site titles; keep size out of the title (option/spec) unless it's a distinct SKU like Mini/Jumbo; keep titles under ~60–70 chars with the key term early; don't repeat words between title, variant and set name.

### Cited Findings
- rhode uses lowercase consistently for product and set titles ("the peptide lip treatment set"); retailers re-case it (Sephora "Peptide Lip Treatment"). — [rhode](https://www.rhodeskin.com/products/the-peptide-lip-treatment-set); [Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/product/peptide-lip-treatment-P517486)
- Own sites omit the brand in titles (Tree Hut "Vanilla Shea Sugar Scrub"; Sol de Janeiro "Brazilian Bum Bum Cream"), while retailers prepend it ("Tree Hut Moroccan Rose Shea Sugar Scrub"; "Sol de Janeiro - 8.1 oz Cheirosa 62 Bum Bum Hair & Body Perfume Mist"). — [Tree Hut](https://www.treehutshea.com/products/vanilla-shea-sugar-scrub); [Ulta Tree Hut](https://www.ulta.com/brand/tree-hut); [Ulta SdJ](https://www.ulta.com/p/cheirosa-62-bum-bum-hair-body-perfume-mist-pimprod2043067?sku=2618557)
- Title SEO guidance: main keyword first; avoid keyword stuffing; ~50–70 chars; Shopify reportedly warns >70 chars may truncate; title tag/SEO title is separate from product title and should be checked. — [1Digital](https://www.1digitalagency.com/blog/how-to-write-product-titles-that-rank-seo-formulas-for-shopify-71385/); [Cloudinary](https://cloudinary.com/guides/ecosystems/shopify-product-page-seo); [EasySell](https://easysellapp.com/blogs/wiki/shopify-product-title-seo-optimization); [RankEngine](https://rankengine.app/blog/shopify-product-title-seo)

## 6. Renaming an existing line without losing brand equity

### Takeaway
Leaders rename by **adding** the new name to the existing equity (Sol de Janeiro "Brazilian Crush" → "Brazilian Crush Cheirosa '62" → "Cheirosa 62"), stating clearly "same product, new name", keeping old URLs/slugs or 301-redirecting them, and keeping hero names untouched.

### Cited Findings
- Sol de Janeiro FAQ: the brand "recently updated the name to include 'Cheirosa '62'", the product is the same as the original Brazilian Crush fragrance spray; bottle design also changed with old/new photos shown. The name story (1962 Rio, Girl from Ipanema) was published in a blog post. Old names persist at retailers. — [SdJ Cheirosa 62 mist page](https://soldejaneiro.com/products/cheirosa-62-hair-body-fragrance-mist); [What is Cheirosa '62?](https://soldejaneiro.com/blogs/fragrance/what-is-cheirosa-62); [Jomashop listing (transition name)](https://www.jomashop.com/sol-de-janeiro-brazilian-crush-cheirosa-62-body-mist-mist-810912032569.html); [Walmart old name](https://www.walmart.com/ip/Brazilian-Crush-Cheirosa-62-by-Sol-De-Janeiro-3-oz-Body-Mist/2992285176)

- Technical: use 301 (not 302) redirects from old to new URLs set up at the moment the handle changes; bulk-map with a spreadsheet; avoid redirect chains; monitor 404s for ~2 weeks. (Vendor-blog sources.) — [ShopCircle: redirects on product name change](https://shopcircle.co/blogs/news/shopify-redirects-product-name-change); [ShopCircle: protect traffic when renaming top seller](https://shopcircle.co/blogs/news/protect-traffic-rename-top-selling-product); [AdNabu 301 guide](https://blog.adnabu.com/shopify-redirects/shopify-301-redirects/)

### Inferences
- Phased rename playbook for a'mie: (1) keep hero/best-seller names; (2) where a rename is needed, use a bridge period "New Name (formerly Old Name)" in title or subtitle/description; (3) on Zid, since product renames change URLs, verify whether Zid auto-creates redirects — if not, prefer changing only displayed/SEO fields or set redirects manually; (4) rename in waves (lowest-traffic first), with backup export before each wave; (5) update ads, social bios, influencer links and GEO/AI-facing copy so old and new names are linked in text (helps search/AI associate them).
- Sol de Janeiro's approach shows "additive" renaming (append signature, later drop old part) preserves recognition better than an abrupt swap.
