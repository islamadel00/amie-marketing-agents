# a'mie product-name audit and brand-equity evidence

Scope: the 46 product names in `00_current_names.md` (Zid, 2026-10-22). Research only; nothing was changed anywhere.

Sources used:
- **[00]** = `/home/user/research_notes/شجرة أسماء منتجات ايمي/00_current_names.md` (current names, prices, published flag)
- **[PMC]** = `/home/user/amie-marketing-agents/.claude/product-marketing.md` (brand facts, KSA keyword volumes, top sellers Jul–Oct 2026)
- **[REV]** = Zid product-reviews export, read-only (`ProductReviews list`, 7 pages, 712 reviews reported, 700 parsed, dated 2022-07-28 to 2026-10-20). The review's product name is the name the product had at the time. Customer names are left out on purpose.

## 1. Inconsistencies and problems in current names, grouped by type

### Takeaway
The catalogue has no single naming rule. The brand appears in 6 written forms. It sits in front of, behind or outside the name, or is missing altogether. Size, skin type and "set" wording show up on some products and not others, and about a quarter of Arabic/English pairs do not say the same thing. The worst problems: two products are both called "Summer Set" (#29, #32); "Glow Set" and "Into The Glow" (#9 vs #1) are near-duplicates; the shade is spelled "Marshmello" (#6, #14) while brand facts say Marshmallow; and four live items have no English name at all.

### Cited Findings (product numbers from [00])

**A. Brand spelling (6 forms)**
- `a'mie` (straight apostrophe): #1, #8 (EN "a'musk"), #12 — [00]
- `a’mie` (curly apostrophe): #2, #14, #21, #22, #26, #28, #34, #35, #36 — [00]
- `A'mi`: #27 EN "A'mi Bag" (the AR name of the same product is "شنطة ايمي بني"). This is likely a typo, and the EN name drops "brown". — [00]
- `a'musk`: #8, a brand-pun sub-brand (AR "مسك ايمي") — [00]
- AR `ايمي`: #8, #12, #14, #22, #26, #27, #28, #34, #35, #36, #43 — [00]
- AR `ايمى` (alif maqsura): #21 "مرآة ايمى" — [00]
- Latin `a'mie` inside an AR name: #1 "مجموعة لمعة الجسم من a'mie", #2 "واكس الشعر من a’mie" — [00]
- Customers write the brand as **"Ami"** in Latin ("كريم Ami كان مناسب بشرتي", "شكرا Ami 🤎") and as "ايمي" in Arabic (43 comments; 1 "إيمي"; 0 "ايمى"; 0 "amie"). — [REV]

**B. Brand position**
- Brand as prefix: AR #14, #22, #26-style "ايمي X"; EN #2, #12, #14, #21, #22, #26, #28, #34–36 — [00]
- Brand as suffix: AR #8 "مسك ايمي", #12 "فرشاة سليك ايمي", #21, #26, #27; "من a'mie" in #1, #2 — [00]
- No brand: everything else (≈30 items) — [00]
- The same product shape is treated differently: the single highlighters carry the brand (#14 "ايمي مارشميلو…", #22 "ايمي تشوكلت…"), but their sets do not (#6, #15). The single Hair Wax carries it (#2), but the single Mascara (#16) does not. — [00]

**C. Spelling and transliteration of shades and ingredients**
- "Marshmello" (#6, #14) vs "Marshmallow" in brand facts — [00], [PMC]
- AR "مارشميلو" and "تشوكلت" are informal transliterations. Customers write both "شوكلت" and "تشوكلت" (5 mentions each pattern). — [REV]
- "الليمون قراس" (#13, #17, #20) is a Gulf colloquial spelling of lemongrass. Other spellings exist (e.g. ليمون جراس / عشبة الليمون), and its search volume was not checked (gap). — [00]
- "مجموعة اللمعه" (#9) ends in ه instead of ة, unlike "لمعة الجسم" (#5) — [00]
- "مجموعة  اليدين والقدمين" (#38) has a double space; "مجموعة العودة للروتين اليومي " (#19) has stray quotation marks and a trailing space. — [00]

**D. Size in name**
- Size in both AR and EN: #16 (15 مل), #30 (150 مل), #31 (120 مل), #33 (200 مل), #37 (10 مل) — [00]
- Size in EN only: #2 (30ml), #17 (300ml), #20 (250ml) — [00]
- No size: the same product families in other variants. Lavender scrub #18 and butter #24 have no 300/250 ml, while their Lemongrass twins #17 and #20 do. Hand Cream #39, Foot Cream #7, Body Glow #5, a'musk #8 (15 ml per [PMC]) and the mists #10 and #11 (150 ml) also have none. — [00], [PMC]
- A size change caused a complaint: "انصدمت الحجم صغروه جدًا صار ٣٠ مل". Putting size in the name exposes reformulations or downsizing. — [REV]

**E. "للبشرة الجافة" (for dry skin) used unevenly**
- AR only, on all Lavender and Lemongrass items: #4, #13, #17, #18, #20, #24. It is never in the EN names. — [00]
- It is missing from the Foot Cream (#7), Hand Cream (#39) and Hand & Foot set (#38), which are also dryness products per [PMC].
- It is a skin-type descriptor, not a medical claim. It does lengthen AR titles (#20 is 6 words).

**F. Arabic and English not parallel (meaning differs)**
- #1 AR "مجموعة لمعة الجسم" (Body Glow Collection) vs EN "Into The Glow Collection": the signature name exists only in EN — [00]
- #2 AR has no size; EN has 30ml — [00]
- #4/#13 AR "مجموعة اللافندر/الليمون قراس للبشرة الجافة" vs EN "Lavender Set" / "Lemongrass Set" — [00]
- #8 AR "مسك ايمي" (a'mie musk) vs EN "a'musk": the pun does not translate — [00]
- #17/#20 EN "Sea Salt Body Scrub 300ml" / "Whipped Body Butter 250ml" (rich descriptors) vs #18/#24 EN "Lavender scrub" / "Lavender Butter" (bare). Twin products get unequal EN names. — [00]
- #21 AR "مرآة ايمى" vs EN "a’mie Fluid-Shaped Luxury Mirror" (EN adds "Fluid-Shaped Luxury") — [00]
- #27 AR "شنطة ايمي بني" vs EN "A'mi Bag" (colour lost) — [00]
- #29 AR "مجموعة الصيف" / #32 AR "الصيف": both EN "Summer Set" — [00]
- #30 AR "زيت تان ديب تان" says "tan" twice; EN "Deep Tan Tanning Oil" says it twice as well — [00]
- #43–46 have no EN name captured — [00]

**G. Sets vs singles, word for "set"**
- AR uses "مجموعة" throughout, but EN mixes "Collection" (#1), "Set" (most) and "Hand and Foot Cream Set" (#38). — [00]
- How sets are built varies. Some are named by scent ("Lavender Set"), some by function ("Hair Set", "Tanning Set"), some by season or occasion ("Summer Set", "Back to Routine Set"), and some by product ("Marshmello Body Highlighter Set"). — [00]
- Duplicate or overlapping set names: "Summer Set" ×2 (#29 live 507 SAR, #32 unpublished 637 SAR); "Tanning Set" (#40, unpublished) vs "Summer Set" (#29, same three tanning products per [PMC]); "Glow Set" (#9) vs "Into The Glow Collection" (#1) vs "Body Glow" (#5) vs "جل اللمعة" (#46). — [00], [PMC]
- Reviews show the problem in practice: a customer under "مجموعة التان" wrote "طلبت مجموعة التشميس". The legacy name "مجموعة الجسم ناتورا" also appears, as do variant-suffixed names such as "مجموعة الصيف - عطر فلورا" and "مجموعة العودة للروتين اليومي - عطر ناتورا". Scent options are added as variant suffixes, which makes names longer. — [REV]

**H. Favours (توزيعات)**
- The pattern is "توزيعات ايمي — X 10 حبات" with an em dash (#28, #34, #35), but #43 uses a hyphen "-" and "5 حبات". — [00]
- EN lowercase is inconsistent: "Musk" vs "hand cream" vs "lip plump", and word order is odd ("10 pieces Musk"). — [00]
- #35 EN "lip plump" names a balm. [PMC] forbids plumping/enlarging claims, yet "Lip Plump" (#35, #37) is the product name itself. Flag for the reviewer: the name implies the effect the rules forbid claiming.
- Favour bags #44/#45 use parentheses "( الكبير )" with spaces inside. — [00]

**I. Accessories**
- #12 "Sleek Brush" is branded and has a sub-name; #23 "Makeup brush" and #25 "Dry body brush" are generic and lowercase. — [00]
- #23 is a highlighter/body brush (per [PMC], highlighter sets include a makeup brush), but "فرشاة الميك اب" suggests face makeup. — [PMC]
- Bags: #26 "a’mie Bag" vs #27 "A'mi Bag" (brown). The colour appears only in AR. — [00]

**J. Capitalisation (EN)**
- Title Case is used on most names, but sentence case appears on #14 "body highlighter", #18 "scrub", #23, #25 and #34/#35. — [00]

**K. Unpublished and legacy names still carrying reviews**
- #42 "واكس التشميس" (Tanning Wax, unpublished) has **44 reviews, avg 4.59**. #40 "مجموعة التان" has 29 reviews (4.28) and #41 "زيت التشميس" 15 (4.4). Their equity sits on unpublished URLs. — [REV]
- Retired names with reviews: "مجموعة الجسم فلورا بلس" (9), "مجموعة الجسم ناتورا" (6), "جل استحمام ناتورا", "جل استحمام معطر - فلورا بلس", "كريم فلورا بلس", "كريم جسم ناتورا". Some include restock pleas: "We need it back please 😭 restock please". — [REV]

### Inferences
- The biggest structural fixes: one Latin brand form (a'mie, straight apostrophe) and one AR form (ايمي); a fixed slot order such as [Line/Signature] + [Product type] + [Variant] + [Size]; one word for set in each language; and size on all singles or on none.
- Removing "للبشرة الجافة" from product names and moving it to descriptions or a category would shorten AR titles. It should be checked against search intent first, since "سكراب للبشرة الجافة" demand was not measured (gap).

### Gaps
- EN names for #43–46 were not captured in [00].
- Keyword volumes for spelling variants (مارشميلو vs مارشملو; ليمون قراس vs ليمون جراس; لمعة vs لمعه) were not checked; this needs Semrush/Keyword Planner.

## 2. Which names carry brand equity (recognition and demand)

### Takeaway
No public web or social footprint could be verified: search indexes return nothing for a'mie product names, and TikTok and the site were blocked. The equity evidence is internal. **Hair Wax ("واكس" / "الواكس")** is by far the strongest: 240 of 700 reviews and #1 by orders. Next come the **scent names Natura and Flora**, which have a loyal following, repeat buyers and restock pleas, and **a'musk / "مسك ايمي"**. The tanning names "واكس التشميس" and "مجموعة التان" carry heavy review equity on now-unpublished items. Signature EN names ("Into The Glow", "Sleek Secret", "Red Cherry", "Deep Tan") are top sellers, but customers almost never say them.

### Cited Findings
- Top sellers by orders, Jul–Oct 2026: Hair Wax, Summer Set, Red Cherry, Hair Set, Deep Tan, Into The Glow, Marshmallow Highlighter Set, Sleek Brush, Glow Set, Body Glow, a'musk, Natura. ~6,900 orders Apr–Oct 2026. — [PMC]
- UGC creators perform strongly on TikTok for hair wax, tan and glow (internal note; not independently verifiable here). — [PMC]
- Review counts and average rating by product name (all time): — [REV]
  - واكس الشعر من a’mie 240 (4.41); كريم اليدين 44 (4.27); واكس التشميس 44 (4.59); مجموعة اللافندر 43 (4.58); مسك ايمي 33 (4.00); لمعة الجسم 31 (4.06); مجموعة التان 29 (4.28); كريم القدمين 28 (4.64); مجموعة اليدين والقدمين 26 (4.69); سكراب اللافندر 25 (4.68); عطر الجسم فلورا 19 (4.74); زبدة اللافندر 19 (4.53); عطر جسم ناتورا 18 (4.17); زيت التشميس 15 (4.4)
  - Signature-named items have few reviews: Red Cherry 3, Sleek Secret mascara 2, Deep Tan 1, Glittery Gold 1, Sleek brush 1, Glow Set 2. "Into The Glow" has **0 reviews under that name** (its AR name is "مجموعة لمعة الجسم").
  - Lemongrass butter has 5 reviews averaging **1.0**: a quality problem, not a naming one, but it weakens Lemongrass as a line name.
- Words customers use in 606 non-empty comments: "واكس" 268 (incl. tanning wax), "لافندر" 80, "تشميس" 59 vs "تان" 38, "لمعة/لمعه" 42, "مسك" 30, "زبدة/زبده" 35, "سكراب" 26, "فلورا" 26, "ناتورا" 21, "هايلايتر" 13, "مارشميلو" 8, "ريد/تشيري" 3, "سليك/Sleek" 4, "ماسكارا" 1, "ديب" 1, "glow/قلو" 3. In hair-wax reviews customers say "واكس"/"الواكس" (250×) and "ستيك" (2×), never "مثبت". — [REV]
- Word of mouth around the wax: "طلبته على مدح صديقتي", "طلبته بناء على مدح أختي… يكفي انه براند سعودي", "حبيييت طلع فعلا على المدح". — [REV]
- Natura has scent equity: "من احبى واميز الروائح… ارجعو وفروه بلييييز", "سمعت مدح عليه من زمان", "عطر ناتورا خيالي". Body Glow reviews ask to buy "نفس العطر" (the Natura scent per [PMC]). — [REV]
- Flora has repeat buyers: "خامس مره اطلبه… اتمنى يرجع يتوفر", "هذا عطري مفضل". — [REV]
- a'musk / مسك ايمي is polarising (avg 4.0). Fans say "المسك الوحيد اللي استخدمه بشكل متكرر" and "اجمل مسك… مختلف عن المسك التقليدي"; detractors complain it is light and oily. Customers say "المسك" / "مسك ايمي" and never "a'musk". — [REV]
- Tanning wax has strong equity: "المنتج الوحيد اللي قدر انه يصبغني… مافي شي مماثل للواكس", "ثاني مره اطلبه", "اطلب اكثر من سنتين ونص". — [REV]
- Influencer mention: one Marshmallow highlighter review names creator **ياسمين الدخيل**: "ياسمين الدخيل مدحت فيه… عادي". This is evidence of influencer seeding for the highlighter. A 2025 Saudi news item describes her as a content creator with an Instagram following. — [REV]; [slaati.com](https://slaati.com/2025/07/03/p2712850.html)
- Category search demand (KSA monthly): مسك 49,500; مرطب شفايف 18,100; توزيعات 18,100; مثبت شعر 14,800; تان 12,100; واكس 8,100; هايلايتر 4,400; واكس للشعر 3,600; لمعة الجسم 1,300. — [PMC]

### Inferences
- **Must preserve** (strongest evidence): "واكس الشعر" (the product-type words customers use), the scent names **Natura** and **Flora**, and **مسك ايمي** (AR). The tanning-wax heritage should also be kept: if Red Cherry cream is its successor (unconfirmed), its old reviews are on unpublished #42.
- **Signature EN names are seller-side, not customer-side.** "Into The Glow", "Sleek Secret", "Red Cherry" and "Deep Tan" sell well (likely through ads and creators) but almost never appear in customer language. They can be kept as line names, but each should sit next to the generic AR type word customers search (تان، ماسكارا شعر، لمعة الجسم).
- "مثبت شعر" (14,800) has more search volume than "واكس للشعر" (3,600), but customers never use it. That makes it a good keyword for descriptions or SEO, not a reason to rename.
- Customers write the brand as "Ami", which suggests a'mie's apostrophe and "e" are not memorable in Latin script. This is one data point (2 comments) and should not drive a rebrand.

### Gaps
- No TikTok, Instagram or Snapchat hashtag/view counts: the platforms were blocked and search engines index nothing for these names. Someone should check manually in the apps: #ايمي_واكس, "ايمي واكس", "a'mie" handles, Snapchat public stories.
- No branded-keyword volumes ("ايمي واكس", "واكس ايمي", "ريد تشيري تان"). Semrush/Keyword Planner (database "sa") is needed.
- No third-party review sites (e.g. Trustpilot or Noon listings) were found.

## 3. Names that are confusing or weak for search, and risky wording

### Takeaway
Several names are generic, duplicated or overlapping: "الصيف", Summer Set ×2, Glow Set vs Into The Glow vs Body Glow, and "واكس". There are no medical claims in the names, but "Lip Plump" implies an effect the brand rules forbid claiming, and "للبشرة الجافة" is acceptable as a descriptor.

### Cited Findings
- "الصيف" (#32, unpublished) is a bare season word with no product type, and its EN duplicates #29. — [00]
- "مجموعة اللمعه" (#9) vs "مجموعة لمعة الجسم من a'mie" (#1) vs "لمعة الجسم" (#5) vs "جل اللمعة" (#46): four glow names, and only #1 is branded "Into The Glow" (in EN only). — [00]
- Customers use "لمعة" mostly to describe shine ("اللمعه خفيفه", "اللمعة مو قوية"), so "لمعة" alone is not distinctive. — [REV]
- "واكس" is ambiguous. a'mie used it for both hair wax (#2) and tanning wax (#42), and customers confuse the two in reviews ("الواكس انتبهو منه اذا طبع على مايوه" under the tan set). The 8,100 KSA volume for "واكس" [PMC] very likely includes hair-removal intent (an inference, not measured).
- "تشميس" (59) vs "تان" (38) in reviews: older customers said "تشميس". The current names use "تان", which matches the larger search term (12,100). — [REV], [PMC]
- "فرشاة الميك اب" (#23) says face makeup brush but is sold with body highlighters. — [00], [PMC]
- "Lip Plump" (#35, #37): [PMC] says "no plumping/enlarging claims", yet the name itself says "plump". — [PMC]
- "للبشرة الجافة" is a skin-type descriptor; no "علاج/يعالج" or medical terms appear in any name. — [00]
- "a'musk" (EN) is a pun customers do not use; it reads like "a musk" and is weak as an EN search term. — [REV]
- Practitioner guidance: descriptive names aid findability, while suggestive or coined names build emotion or ownership; mixing types works if the construction is consistent — [Bluetext](https://bluetext.com/blog/name/). Shopify's naming guidelines say names should reveal purpose in plain user terms and reserve evocative names for standalone products — [Shopify Polaris](https://polaris.shopify.com/content/naming).

### Inferences
- The fix is probably a consistent "evocative line name + plain product type" pattern (e.g. "Into The Glow + زيت لمعة الجسم"), not removing signature names.
- "Summer Set" vs "Tanning Set" and "Glow Set" vs "Into The Glow" should each collapse to one clear name, without renaming URLs: change the visible title rather than the slug, per CLAUDE.md, renaming in Zid changes URL; confirm before any action.

### Gaps
- Whether "Lip Plump" is acceptable under Saudi SFDA cosmetic-naming rules was not researched.

## 4. Possible name collisions for signature names (flag only, not legal advice)

### Takeaway
**Natura** and **Flora** collide with very large beauty and fragrance names, Natura &Co and Gucci Flora. "Into The Glow", "Red Cherry", "Deep Tan" and "Glittery Gold" are descriptive phrases close to others in the tanning/glow space. No exact-match trademark was found in quick searches, which were not formal trademark searches.

### Cited Findings
- **Natura:** Natura Cosméticos (Brazil, founded 1969) is the largest Brazilian cosmetics company, part of Natura &Co, and present in 73 countries — [Wikipedia: Natura &Co](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natura_%26_Co). Its body splashes are sold in Latin America (e.g. Natura Tododia); no Saudi sale was found in one search — [Mercado Libre listing](https://www.mercadolibre.com.co/shorts/clips/AcomQM/body-splash-purpure-coconut-shine).
- **Flora:** Gucci "Flora" is a major women's fragrance line (launched 2009; Flora Gorgeous Gardenia/Jasmine) with Flora body lotion, cream and shower gel — [perfume.com](https://www.perfume.com/gucci/flora/women-perfume); [FragranceX](https://www.fragrancex.com/products/gucci/flora-perfume); [Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/Flora-Gorgeous-Gardenia-by-Gucci-Eau-De-Toilette-1-6oz-50ml-Spray-New-With-Box/37654018). a'mie's own retired "فلورا بلس" shows the name has been reused in-house too — [REV].
- **Into The Glow:** no exact brand or trademark found. Bali Body (tanning/shimmer brand) runs a blog section titled "In The Glow" — [Bali Body](https://il.balibodyco.com/blogs/in-the-glow/how-to-get-a-seriously-deep-tan-this-summer). Bath & Body Works sells "Into the Night" Glowtion in the UAE — [Bath & Body Works AE](https://www.bathandbodyworks.ae/en/buy-night-glowtion-body-butter-0). "Glow" marks are actively litigated (Glow Recipe vs Makeup by Mario over "Watermelon Glow") — [The Fashion Law](https://www.thefashionlaw.com/glow-recipe-is-suing-makeup-by-mario-over-watermelon-glow-trademark/).
- **Red Cherry:** no tanning brand named "Red Cherry" was found, but cherry-themed tan products sell in KSA: Supre Tan "Cherry Bomb" at Nahdi — [Nahdi](https://www.nahdionline.com/en-sa/supre-tan-cherry-bomb-dark-tan-accelerator-235-ml/pdp/103080681); Carrot Sun "Cherry Boom" — [Medon](https://medononline.com/product/carrot-sun-cherry-boom-tan-cream-350ml-10435).
- **Deep Tan / Glittery Gold:** these are generic descriptors. Beesline Suntan Oil Gold (sold at Nahdi KSA) is marketed with "Deep Tan" and "Shimmering Glow" — [Nahdi](https://www.nahdionline.com/en-sa/beesline-suntan-oil-gold-200-ml/pdp/101802619); Bondi Sands "Liquid Gold" — [LloydsPharmacy](https://lloydspharmacy.com/products/bondi-sands-gradual-tanning-oil-everyday-liquid-gold-270ml.oembed).
- **Sleek Secret:** no exact match found. "Sleek MakeUP" is an established UK cosmetics brand (eye mascaras) — [ASOS](https://www.asos.com/au/sleek/sleek-makeup-full-package-all-in-one-mascara/prd/202559823).
- **a'mie / amie:** search results are dominated by other "Amie" entities (an AI calendar app, a research platform, AMIE certification) — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/amie); [Wikipedia: Amie](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amie). Latin-script "amie" is a crowded term, which also explains the zero indexed results.

### Inferences
- Natura and Flora carry real customer equity (section 2), so the risk is a trade-off. Low-risk mitigations that keep the equity: always pair the scent name with the brand and the product type ("a'mie Natura Body Mist"), and never use either name as a standalone line or brand.
- "Deep Tan" and "Glittery Gold" are descriptive and weak as distinctive marks. "Red Cherry" and "Into The Glow" are more ownable, but sit near competitors' cherry-tan and glow naming.

### Gaps
- No SAIP (Saudi IP Authority), WIPO Global Brand Database or USPTO search was run. A formal clearance search is needed before investing in any signature name.
