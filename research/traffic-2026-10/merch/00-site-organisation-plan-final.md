## A. What confuses or stops shoppers
1. Glow family: 7 options (excluding فرشاة الميك اب), 30,194 views / 1,293 units (GA4). Prices 79 / 99 / 176 / 205 / 294. Growth, not cannibalisation: Zid 9.3 → 28.8 units/day Sep→Oct; GA4 252 → 654 units.
   - مجموعة لمعة الجسم (Into The Glow, 294) saves 0 if its contents are جلّ لمعة الجسم 176 + stick 79 + brush 39 (contents unconfirmed). It costs 19 SAR more than جلّ لمعة الجسم + مجموعة هايلايتر الجسم مارشميللو (275), bought together in 92 orders.
   - Into The Glow cart→unit 16.4% vs store 20.0% (z −3.8, ~65 units short). The loss is in the Marshmello variant (240 units / 1,522 adds = 15.8%) vs Chocolate (54 / 269 = 20.1%).
   - Marshmello = 85% of adds and 81% of Zid units (258 / 319); variant stock 41 vs Chocolate 238 ≈ 4 days of stock at October pace.
   - Names: Into The Glow is "مجموعة لمعة الجسم" on Zid, Glow Set is "مجموعة جلّ لمعة الجسم"; GA4 also logs Glow Set as "مجموعة لمعة الجسم". Body Glow is called "جلّ" but product-facts.md says it is an oily liquid.
2. Singles vs sets 20 SAR apart (79 vs 99). Sets outsell singles ~2:1 (Zid May–Oct: Marshmello 564 vs 279, Chocolate 396 vs 165). هايلايتر الجسم مارشميللو single: 786 adds, 15.6% cart→unit (z −3.1).
3. Scrub/butter (16,669 views / 407 units): Lemongrass Set add-to-cart 7.1% vs Lavender Set 21.7%. Cluster flat (6.2 → 6.1 units/day) while Lavender (0 → 3.9/day) replaces Lemongrass (6.2 → 2.2/day).
4. Discounts: every strike-through is a 4.6–5% set discount. Real savings vs singles: 0% Into The Glow; 5% Hair / Lemongrass / Summer / Glow sets; 14% Lavender Set; 16% highlighter sets. Coupons on 48.5% of orders May–Oct (80.6% Sep, 71.1% Oct).
5. Wrong or missing content: مجموعة الصيف (EN text describes a face cleanser, 1 image, meta promises a fragrance), مجموعة الليمون قراس (SEO title promises a dry brush), مجموعة الشعر (shows 2 of 4 items; mascara block describes wax), مجموعة لمعة الجسم (no shade guide, 0 reviews shown, no EN copy, contents unconfirmed).
6. Views with no way to buy (out of stock, 0–4 adds): كريم اليدين للترطيب, مجموعة القدمين و اليدين, مرطب الشفاه بالعسل, بوكس ايمي للاهداء, favors.
7. Badges that contradict stock: "Selling fast" on out-of-stock كريم تان رد تشيري, on سكراب اللافندر (857 in stock) and مجموعة الصيف (24 units in 30 days); "Limited Quantity" on مجموعة العودة للروتين (728 in stock).
8. Paid traffic on weak pages and duplicate URLs: Paid Social view→unit مرآة ايمى 0.15%, مجموعة الليمون قراس 0.41%, مجموعة الصيف 0.58%. Trailing-slash copies (hair category, Summer Set, tan category): 197 adds / 15,539 sessions (1.3%) vs 2,648 / 21,983 (12.0%) clean. 402 of the top 500 GSC pages are country-prefix copies.
