## Patterns that repeat across the high-end sites
1. One backdrop tone for all product photos.
2. Hero = one product or one tight group, large, with a short headline and one button.
3. Three to five category tiles right under the hero.
4. Product cards with a full-width add-to-cart button and no decoration.
5. Two typefaces at most, large headings, light body text.
6. Off-white or warm page colour, not pure white, on about half of them.
