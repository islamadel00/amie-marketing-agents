# a'mie: برومبتات صور الموقع (جاهزة للنسخ في أي شات)

## إزاي تستخدم الملف ده
1. افتح الشات اللي بيولّد صور.
2. ارفع صور الرفرنس المكتوبة تحت كل برومبت (من ملف `amie-product-refs.zip`).
3. انسخ **«بلوك الستايل الثابت»** مرة واحدة في أول الشات.
4. بعدها انسخ برومبت اللقطة اللي عايزها.
5. صورة واحدة في كل طلب. راجع شكل العبوة والكلام اللي عليها قبل ما تعتمد.

---

## بلوك الستايل الثابت (يتنسخ في أول الشات)

```
You are generating website images for a'mie, a Saudi premium body-care brand.
Follow these rules in every image.

LOOK
- High-end editorial product photography, calm and minimal, quiet luxury.
- Soft warm daylight from the upper left, gentle long shadows, no harsh flash.
- Real photographic texture: fine grain, true materials, shallow depth of field.
- Lots of clean negative space. One clear subject per image.

COLOUR PALETTE (use only these tones)
- Background, warm rose ivory: #F6F0EB
- Product backdrop, soft warm beige: #EADFD7
- Deep brown for dark sections: #3A2522
- a'mie brown: #5E2E21
- Burgundy brown accent: #663C3C
- Warm bronze glow accent, small details only: #B98B6E
- No red, orange, green, blue or pure white. No neon, no gradients, no gold glitter.

PROPS (sparingly)
- Travertine or warm stone slab, ivory linen, clear water drops, a ceramic dish in beige.
- No flowers bouquets, no fruit, no candles, no busy scenes.

PRODUCT ACCURACY (most important)
- Copy every product exactly from the attached reference photo: same shape, proportions,
  colours, cap, logo position and label layout. Do not redesign the packaging.
- Do not invent label text. Where text is too small to read, keep it tiny and soft.
- The brand name is written exactly: a'mie (lowercase, with apostrophe).
- Do not add any text, price, discount, badge, watermark or logo outside the product itself.

PEOPLE (only when the prompt asks)
- Gulf / Saudi woman, natural skin texture, modest styling, hands and shoulders only
  unless told otherwise. Natural hands with five fingers, short neutral nails.
```

## 1) هيرو الموبايل (سلايدر زد)
**المقاس:** 2:3 عمودي (1080×1620)
**الرفرنس:** `lavender-set.jpg` (أو صورة المنتج اللي عايزه في الهيرو)

```
Vertical 2:3 hero image for a mobile website slider.
The Lavender Set from image 1 (clear glass salt scrub jar with blush-pink lid, opaque
blush-pink whipped body butter jar, round wooden dry body brush with jute rope handle),
exactly as in the reference, arranged on a warm travertine slab.
Background: seamless warm rose ivory #F6F0EB fading softly to #EADFD7.
Soft daylight from upper left, long gentle shadows, a few clear water drops on the stone.
Products sit in the lower half of the frame. The top 40% is empty clean background
for a headline. Leave the top 12% completely empty (site header overlaps it).
Calm, premium, editorial. No text in the image.
```

## 2) هيرو الديسكتوب
**المقاس:** 16:9 أفقي (1920×1080)
**الرفرنس:** نفس صورة المنتج

```
Horizontal 16:9 hero image for a desktop website.
Same products as image 1, exactly as in the reference, grouped on the right third of the
frame on a warm travertine slab. The left two thirds are empty clean background in warm
rose ivory #F6F0EB for a headline and a button.
Soft daylight from upper left, gentle shadows falling to the right.
No visible seam or line in the background. No text in the image.
```

## 3) صورة المنتج لكارت المنتج (خلفية موحّدة)
**المقاس:** 3:4 عمودي (1200×1600)
**الرفرنس:** صورة المنتج من الأمام (مثلًا `body-glow-front.jpg`)

```
Vertical 3:4 studio packshot for a product card.
One product only: the product from image 1, exactly the same shape, colours, pump/cap and
label layout as the reference, standing upright, centred, straight front view.
Flat seamless background in soft warm beige #EADFD7, with a very soft contact shadow under
the product. Even soft light, no reflections of the studio, no props.
The product fills about 60% of the frame height. No text added.
```

## 4) لقطة القوام (ماكرو) لبانر الموسم
**المقاس:** 16:9 أفقي، ونسخة 4:5 للموبايل
**الرفرنس:** `lavender-body-butter.jpg` أو `lavender-body-scrub.jpg`

```
Macro texture shot, horizontal 16:9.
A smooth swirl of white whipped body butter on a warm beige ceramic dish, shot very close,
soft peaks and silky folds, light catching the surface.
The jar from image 1 sits out of focus in the back right corner, exactly as in the reference.
Background in warm rose ivory #F6F0EB. Soft daylight, shallow depth of field.
Left half of the frame stays calm and empty for a short headline. No text in the image.
```

للسكراب بدّل السطر التاني بـ:
`A scoop of pale pink sea salt scrub with darker specks on a warm stone surface, grains glistening with oil, shot very close.`

## 5) كارت القسم (تسوّقي حسب روتينك)
**المقاس:** 1:1 مربع (1200×1200)
**الرفرنس:** صورة منتج القسم (مثلًا `hair-set-products.jpg` للعناية بالشعر)

```
Square 1:1 category tile on a dark background.
The products from image 1 (matte black hair wax stick, ivory hair mascara tube, slim black
brush), exactly as in the reference, standing together slightly off-centre.
Background: deep chocolate brown #3A2522, matte, with a soft warm light from the upper left
creating a gentle glow behind the products. Warm bronze #B98B6E rim light on the edges only.
Moody, premium, minimal. Lower third stays simple for a short white label. No text in the image.
```

## 6) لقطة لايف ستايل بالإيد
**المقاس:** 4:5 عمودي (1080×1350)
**الرفرنس:** `hand-cream-3-in-use-SPF10-1200x1600.jpg` وصورة المنتج من الأمام

```
Vertical 4:5 lifestyle image.
A Gulf woman's hand resting on ivory linen, holding the product from image 1 exactly as in
the reference. Keep the hand position and grip from image 2. Natural skin texture, five
fingers, short neutral nails, a thin fine ring at most.
Background: warm rose ivory #F6F0EB, soft morning daylight from the left, shallow depth of field.
Calm, intimate, premium. The label may be partly hidden by the fingers. No text added.
```

## 7) لقطة المجموعة مع الشنطة (للهدايا)
**المقاس:** 4:5 عمودي
**الرفرنس:** صورة المجموعة و`shopping-bag-real.jpg`

```
Vertical 4:5 gift image.
The set from image 1, exactly as in the reference, arranged in front of the a'mie shopping
bag from image 2: white matte paper bag, white twisted rope handles, a'mie logo in silver foil,
centred. Do not change the bag colour or handles.
Surface: warm travertine. Background: soft warm beige #EADFD7.
Soft daylight from upper left. Elegant, minimal, ready-to-gift mood. No ribbon, no text added.
```

## لو النتيجة طلعت غلط: جُمل تصحيح جاهزة
- العبوة اتغيّرت: `Redo. The packaging must match image 1 exactly: same shape, colours, cap and label layout. Do not redesign it.`
- كلام غريب على العبوة: `Remove invented text. Keep only the label text that is visible in the reference; make small text tiny and soft.`
- الألوان خرجت عن الثيم: `Shift the whole image to the palette: background #F6F0EB / #EADFD7, accents #5E2E21 and #B98B6E only. Remove any other colour.`
- الصورة شكلها AI: `Make it look like a real photograph: natural grain, real material texture, softer light, less contrast, no glow halos.`
- مفيش مكان للعنوان: `Move the products lower/right and leave the top 40% as clean empty background.`
