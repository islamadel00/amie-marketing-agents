Lemongrass Sea Salt Body Scrub

Effective Exfoliation. Noticeably Smoother Skin.

A body scrub combining sea salt and crushed nutshell particles to exfoliate dead skin cells and smooth rough areas. Shea and mango butters, along with moisturizing oils, help maintain skin softness after exfoliation. Finished with a refreshing lemongrass scent, it leaves skin feeling smooth and renewed.

Key Ingredients & Benefits+

Sea Salt

Helps exfoliate and remove dead skin cells.

Crushed Nutshells

Exfoliating particles that help smooth rough areas and refine skin texture.

Shea & Mango Butters

Help moisturize and maintain skin softness.

Argan Oil

Helps nourish skin and reduce dryness.

Rosemary Oil

Contributes to skin care.

Vitamin E

An antioxidant that helps protect skin.

How to Use+

Apply to wet skin during a shower or bath. Massage gently in circular motions, focusing on rough areas, then rinse thoroughly with water.

Recommended once or twice a week. Avoid use on irritated or damaged skin.
