Chocolate Body Highlighter

Soft, Even Bronze Shimmer

A body highlighter with a warm bronze shimmer that reflects light and enhances the skin’s appearance without changing its underlying tone. Its creamy, easy-to-blend texture delivers an even glow that can be built up to the desired intensity, for everyday looks and special occasions.

Key Ingredients & Benefits+

Shea Butter

Helps moisturize skin and maintain softness.

Cocoa Butter

Helps soften skin and reduce dryness.

Paraffin

Helps give the formula a consistent, easy-to-apply texture.

How to Use+

Apply after getting dressed, on clean, dry skin. Glide the highlighter over the desired areas of the body, then blend thoroughly with a body brush for an even finish.

Build up gradually for a more noticeable shimmer. Allow the product to dry completely before it comes into contact with clothing.

Removal: Wash off with soap and water.

Ingredients+

Shea Butter, Cocoa Butter, Paraffin, Bronze Pigments, Shimmer Particles.
