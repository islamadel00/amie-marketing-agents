Body Brush

Even Application. A Refined Finish.

A body brush designed to apply and blend glow products evenly across the skin for a smooth, streak-free finish. Its soft, dense bristles allow for easy control and seamless blending.

Brush Features+

Soft, dense bristles for even application.

Helps blend body highlighter without visible streaks.

Suitable for use with a’mie Body Glow and Chocolate and Marshmallow Body Highlighters.

Practical design for easy control during application.

How to Use+

Apply a suitable amount of product to the skin, then gently blend with the brush until the glow looks even.

Brush Care+

Gently wash the bristles with lukewarm water and a makeup brush cleanser. Allow to dry completely before the next use.
