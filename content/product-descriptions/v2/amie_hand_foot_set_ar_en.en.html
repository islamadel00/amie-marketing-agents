Hand & Foot Care Set

Complete daily care for hands and feet

A set combining hand cream and foot cream, with two moisture-rich formulas designed to care for dry, rough skin and help maintain softness and suppleness.

Hand Cream | 75 ml

Enriched with shea butter, aloe vera, and glycerin to moisturize hands and keep them soft. Features a fast-absorbing texture, the Natura fragrance with coconut and vanilla notes, and SPF 10 sun protection.

Foot Cream | 75 ml

Enriched with urea and lactic acid to moisturize feet, soften rough areas, and improve the appearance of dryness-related cracks, leaving skin feeling softer and more supple.

How to Use+

Hand Cream: Apply an appropriate amount to the hands and massage until absorbed. Reapply as needed, especially after washing hands.

Foot Cream: Apply to clean, dry feet, focusing on heels and rough areas. Massage until absorbed. Use daily.

Ingredients+

Hand Cream: Shea butter, aloe vera, glycerin, moisturizing oils, Natura fragrance.

Foot Cream: Moisturizing oils, panthenol, lactic acid, glycerin, urea, vitamin E.

Set Includes: Hand Cream 75 ml + Foot Cream 75 ml.
