Marshmallow Body Highlighter

Soft, Even Pearlescent Glow

A body highlighter with a light pearlescent shimmer that reflects light and gives skin a luminous finish without changing its natural tone. Its creamy, easy-to-blend texture delivers an even glow that can be built up as desired, suitable for different skin tones, everyday looks, and special occasions.

Key Ingredients & Benefits+

Shea Butter

Helps moisturize skin and maintain softness.

Cocoa Butter

Helps soften skin and reduce dryness.

Paraffin

Helps provide a consistent texture for easy application.

How to Use+

Apply after getting dressed, onto clean, dry skin. Glide the highlighter over desired areas and blend thoroughly with a body brush for an even glow. Layer gradually for a more noticeable finish. Allow to dry completely before contact with clothing.

Removal: Wash off with soap and water.

Ingredients+

Shea butter, cocoa butter, paraffin, pearlescent pigments, shimmer particles.
