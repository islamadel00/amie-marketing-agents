Hair Mascara

The Final Touch for a Polished Look

Sleek Secret Hair Mascara features a lightweight formula and a precision brush to help manage short, flyaway hair after styling, leaving a neat, polished look with a natural finish. Ideal for finishing touches and quick touch-ups, in a convenient size to carry and use anytime.

Key Ingredients & Benefits+

Styling polymers (PVP)

Help hold short, flyaway hair in place.

Panthenol

Helps moisturize hair and improve softness.

Glycerin

Helps retain moisture in the hair.

Propylene Glycol

Helps maintain moisture in the formula and support even application.

How to Use+

After styling, gently brush over short, flyaway hair to smooth it into place. Reapply as needed.
