Rich Moisture. Lasting Softness.

A formula enriched with shea butter, aloe vera, and glycerin to help moisturize hands, protect against dryness, and maintain skin softness and suppleness. Its fast-absorbing, non-greasy texture is complemented by the Natura fragrance, blending coconut and vanilla, and SPF 10 sun protection. The convenient size makes it easy to carry in your bag and use anytime.

Shea Butter
