Body Glow

Dewy Glow with a Silky Finish

a’mie Body Glow delivers a soft, even sheen that reflects light and enhances the skin’s natural appearance, without visible glitter particles. Its lightweight, easy-to-blend formula leaves skin looking dewy and feeling silky, without a greasy finish.

Suitable for all skin tones, whether naturally untanned or tanned, it enhances the skin’s glow without changing its color or making it appear darker. Scented with Natura, featuring coconut and vanilla notes, it is ideal for everyday wear and special occasions.
