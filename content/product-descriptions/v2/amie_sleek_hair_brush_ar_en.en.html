a’mie Sleek Hair Brush

Precise Styling for a Sleek, Polished Look

A densely bristled brush designed to smooth hair and brush it back for a sleek, polished finish. Its pointed handle allows precise sectioning and parting, making it ideal for sleek, pulled-back hairstyles.

Brush Features+

Dense bristles help gather and smooth hair while styling.

Pointed handle for precise sectioning and parting.

Ideal for slicked-back and updo hairstyles.

Can be used with a’mie Hair Wax for enhanced hold.

How to Use+

Brush hair in the desired styling direction, using the pointed end to section and part the hair. Pair with a’mie Hair Wax to help hold hair in place and maintain a polished look.

Brush Care+

Clean the bristles regularly to remove styling-product residue, and allow the brush to dry completely before the next use.
