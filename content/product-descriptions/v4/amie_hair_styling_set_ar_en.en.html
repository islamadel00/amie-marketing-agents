a’mie Hair Styling Set

Hair Styling Set

Hair styling essentials in one set

A complete set featuring Hair Wax, Sleek Secret Hair Mascara and a styling brush, together with an a’mie bag for easy organization and carrying. Designed to help style hair, manage short and flyaway strands, and maintain a polished look throughout the day.

Set Includes+

Hair Wax | 30 g

Formulated with beeswax and sweet almond oil to help hold hair in place and smooth it back for a polished finish with natural shine.

Sleek Secret Hair Mascara | 15 ml

A lightweight formula with a precision brush that helps manage short and flyaway hair after styling for a neat, natural-looking finish.

Hair Styling Brush

A dense-bristle brush for smoothing and brushing hair back, with a pointed handle for precise parting and sectioning.

a’mie Bag

A practical bag for organizing and carrying the set while traveling or on the go.

How to Use+

Apply Hair Wax to dry or damp hair, then use the styling brush to distribute it and shape the hair in the desired direction.

After styling, use Sleek Secret Hair Mascara as a finishing touch to manage short and flyaway hair and make precise touch-ups.

Key Ingredients+

Hair Wax

Beeswax, sweet almond oil, vitamin E.

Sleek Secret Hair Mascara

Fixing polymers (PVP), panthenol, glycerin, propylene glycol.

Set contents

Hair Wax 30 g + Hair Mascara 15 ml + Hair Styling Brush + a’mie Bag.
