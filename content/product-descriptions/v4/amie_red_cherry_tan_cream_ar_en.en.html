Warm Red-Toned Tan

A tanning cream designed to help develop a warm, red-toned tan during sun exposure, for a rich, even sun-kissed appearance.

Its creamy, oil-rich formula helps keep skin soft and reduce dryness while tanning.

Key Ingredients & Benefits+

Coconut Oil

Helps moisturize and soften skin.

Olive Oil

Helps maintain skin softness and reduce dryness.

Carrot Oil

Contributes to skin care through its naturally occurring components.

Argan Oil

Helps moisturize skin and improve its feel.

Grapeseed Oil

Helps soften skin and maintain suppleness.

Beeswax

Apply evenly to clean, dry skin before tanning, ensuring consistent coverage of the desired areas. Reapply as needed during use.
