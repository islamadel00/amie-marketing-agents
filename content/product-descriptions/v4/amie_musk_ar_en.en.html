a’mie Musk

Clean Musk with Warm Floral Notes

An oil-based musk fragrance combining the softness of white musk with the floral notes of ylang-ylang. It leaves the skin with a clean, subtle scent and a warm finish. Ideal for everyday use and after showering, in a convenient, portable dropper bottle.

Fragrance Notes+

White Musk

A soft, clean scent with a warm character.

Ylang-Ylang

Rich floral notes that add depth and balance.

How to Use+

Apply a few drops of a’mie Musk to the body or pulse points, such as the wrists and neck, and gently spread over the skin. Reapply as desired.

Key Fragrance Notes+

White musk, ylang-ylang.

Size: 15 ml
