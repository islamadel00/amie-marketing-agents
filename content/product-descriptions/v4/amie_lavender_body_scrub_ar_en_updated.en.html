Lavender Body Scrub

Effective exfoliation for smoother-feeling skin

A body scrub that helps remove dead skin cells and smooth rough areas, improving skin texture and leaving it noticeably softer. Combines exfoliation with skin care and a gentle lavender scent for a refreshing shower experience.

Key Ingredients & Benefits+

Sea salt and nut shells

Help exfoliate dead skin cells and smooth rough areas.

Shea butter and mango butter

Help moisturize skin and maintain softness.

Argan oil

Helps soften skin. Rosemary oil: Supports skin care. Lavender oil: Provides a floral scent. Vitamin E: An antioxidant.

How to Use+

Apply to damp skin in the shower. Massage gently in circular motions, focusing on rough areas, then rinse thoroughly with water.

Recommended once or twice weekly. Avoid use on irritated or damaged skin.
