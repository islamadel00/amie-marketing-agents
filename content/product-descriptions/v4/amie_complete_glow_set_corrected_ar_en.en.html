Three Glow Finishes for Different Looks

A set featuring Chocolate and Marshmallow Body Highlighters, Body Glow Gel, and a blending brush. Create an even glow and adjust its intensity to suit your look.

What’s Included+

Chocolate Body Highlighter | 30 ml

A creamy highlighter with warm bronze shimmer that enhances the skin without covering its natural tone.

Marshmallow Body Highlighter | 30 ml

A creamy highlighter with soft pearlescent shimmer, suitable for a range of skin tones.

Body Glow Gel | 120 ml

Adds a dewy glow and silky feel without changing skin tone, with the Natura scent of coconut and vanilla.

Body Brush

Designed to blend and distribute the products for a smooth, even finish.

How to Use+

After getting dressed, choose Chocolate for a warm bronze shimmer or Marshmallow for a soft pearlescent glow.

Apply the highlighter to clean, dry skin and blend thoroughly with the brush. Allow it to dry and set briefly, then apply Body Glow Gel with the brush as the finishing touch for enhanced shine and a dewy-looking finish.

Removal: Wash off the highlighter with soap and water.
