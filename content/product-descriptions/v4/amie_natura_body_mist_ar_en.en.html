Natura Body Mist

A warm blend of coconut and vanilla

Natura Body Mist combines coconut and vanilla with soft floral notes, complemented by warm touches of musk and tonka. Its light fragrance leaves the body and hair delicately scented, ideal for everyday use and refreshing throughout the day.

Fragrance Notes+

Top notes

Pink pepper and coconut.

Heart notes

Ylang-ylang and tuberose.

Base notes

Vanilla, tonka and musk.

How to Use+

Spray onto the body or hair from a suitable distance. Reapply as desired to refresh the scent. Avoid contact with eyes.

Key Fragrance Notes+

Coconut, vanilla, pink pepper, ylang-ylang, tuberose, tonka, musk.

Size: 150 ml
