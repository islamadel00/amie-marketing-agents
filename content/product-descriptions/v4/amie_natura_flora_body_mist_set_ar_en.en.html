Natura & Flora Body Mist Set

Two distinct scents for different moments

A set featuring Natura and Flora body mists, offering two distinct fragrances for everyday use on the body and hair. Natura blends warm coconut and vanilla notes, while Flora brings together refreshing citrus and white florals.

Set Contents+

Natura Body Mist | 150 ml

A warm blend of coconut and vanilla with soft floral notes and touches of musk and tonka. Ideal for those who prefer warm, balanced scents.

Flora Body Mist | 150 ml

A refreshing floral fragrance combining citrus and white flowers with soft musk and woody notes. Ideal for those who prefer clean, light scents.

How to Use+

Spray onto the body or hair from a suitable distance. Reapply as desired to refresh the scent. Avoid contact with eyes.

Fragrance Notes+

Natura

Coconut, vanilla, pink pepper, ylang-ylang, tuberose, tonka, musk.

Flora

Citrus, white flowers, lily of the valley, musk, soft woods.

Set includes: Natura Body Mist 150 ml + Flora Body Mist 150 ml.
