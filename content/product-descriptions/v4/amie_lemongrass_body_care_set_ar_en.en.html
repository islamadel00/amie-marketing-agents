How to Use+

Dry Body Brush: Use before showering on dry skin with gentle strokes, avoiding excessive pressure.

Body Scrub: Apply to damp skin in the shower, massage gently in circular motions, then rinse thoroughly.

Body Butter: Massage onto clean skin after showering until absorbed to maintain moisture and softness.

Exfoliate once or twice weekly depending on skin tolerance. Avoid using the brush and scrub on the same area during a single session to reduce the risk of irritation.
