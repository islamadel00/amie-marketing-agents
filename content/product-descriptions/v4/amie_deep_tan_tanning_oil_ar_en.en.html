a’mie Deep Tan Tanning Oil

Golden Bronze Tan with a Soft Glow

A tanning oil designed to enhance a golden bronze appearance during sun exposure, for a warm, even-looking tan. Its blend of moisturizing oils, shea butter and cocoa butter helps keep skin feeling soft and reduces dryness while tanning.

Key Ingredients & Benefits+

Carrot Oil

Contains beta-carotene and helps condition the skin.

Henna Oil

Included in the tanning formula to enhance the appearance of color.

Coconut & Olive Oils

Help moisturize and soften the skin.

Shea & Cocoa Butters

Help maintain skin moisture and softness.

Vitamin E

An antioxidant that helps care for the skin.

How to Use+

Shake well, then apply evenly over the body before tanning. Reapply as needed, particularly after swimming.

Caution: This product is not a sunscreen. Use a broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher and avoid prolonged sun exposure.

Highlighted Ingredients+

Carrot oil, henna oil, coconut oil, olive oil, shea butter, cocoa butter, vitamin E.
