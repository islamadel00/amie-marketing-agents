a’mie Flora Body Mist

A Fresh Floral Scent with Citrus Notes

Flora Body Mist blends citrus and white florals with soft notes of musk and woods, leaving body and hair with a clean, refreshing scent. Its light, balanced fragrance is ideal for everyday wear and refreshing throughout the day.

Fragrance Notes+

Top notes

Fresh citrus.

Heart notes

White flowers and lily of the valley.

Base notes

Musk and soft woods.

How to Use+

Spray onto the body or hair from a suitable distance. Reapply as desired to refresh the scent. Avoid contact with eyes.

Key Fragrance Notes+

Citrus, white flowers, lily of the valley, musk, woods.
