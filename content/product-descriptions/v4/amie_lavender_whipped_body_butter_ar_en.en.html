Lavender Whipped Body Butter

Rich Moisture with a Soft, Silky Feel

A whipped body butter formulated with shea butter, cocoa butter and moisturizing oils to help hydrate dry skin and improve its texture and suppleness. Its lightweight, easy-to-spread consistency leaves skin feeling soft without a greasy finish, with a gentle lavender scent for everyday body care.

Key Ingredients & Benefits+

Shea Butter & Cocoa Butter

Help moisturize and soften skin while reducing dryness.

Hyaluronic Acid

Helps attract and retain moisture in the skin.

Sweet Almond Oil

Helps soften skin and improve its texture.

Jojoba Oil

Helps maintain skin moisture and softness.

Lavender Oil

Gives the product its distinctive scent.

How to Use+

Apply a suitable amount to clean, dry skin and massage until absorbed, focusing on areas prone to dryness. Use daily, preferably after showering.

Highlighted Ingredients+

Shea butter, cocoa butter, hyaluronic acid, sweet almond oil, jojoba oil, lavender oil.
