a’mie Dry Body Brush

Dry Exfoliation for Smoother-Feeling Skin

A body brush designed for use on dry skin to help remove dead skin cells and soften rough areas, improving the look and feel of the skin. Dense bristles and an easy-to-hold design make it a practical addition to your body-care routine.

Brush Benefits+

Gentle exfoliation

Helps remove dead skin cells and improve skin texture.

Smoother skin

Helps reduce the feel of rough, dry areas, especially on arms and legs.

Easy to use

Practical design offers control over brushing pressure.

How to Use+

Use on dry skin before showering. Brush gently in long, light strokes upward, avoiding excessive pressure or repeated brushing over the same area.

Start once or twice a week, depending on skin tolerance, and moisturize after showering.

Brush Care+

Remove debris from the bristles after use. Clean according to the care instructions for the brush material, then allow it to dry completely in a well-ventilated area.
