How to Use+

Dry Body Brush: Before showering, use on dry skin with gentle strokes without applying excessive pressure.

Body Scrub: Apply to wet skin in the shower, massage gently in circular motions, and rinse thoroughly.

Body Butter: Massage onto clean skin after showering until absorbed.

Exfoliate once or twice weekly according to skin tolerance. Avoid using the brush and scrub on the same area in a single session to minimize irritation.
