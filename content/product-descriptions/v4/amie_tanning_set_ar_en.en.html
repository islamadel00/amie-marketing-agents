Warm Tan Tones with a Soft Golden Shimmer

A set featuring Red Cherry Tan Cream, Deep Tan Tanning Oil, and Glittery Gold Tanning Gel—three distinct formulas for body care during sun exposure. Designed to complement warm and bronzed-looking skin with a soft golden shimmer.

What’s Included+

Red Cherry Tan Cream | 200 ml

A tanning cream designed to support a warm, reddish-toned tan, enriched with moisturizing oils to help keep skin soft.

Deep Tan Tanning Oil | 150 ml

A tanning oil designed to enhance the appearance of a golden-bronze tan, with moisturizing ingredients to help reduce dryness.

Glittery Gold Tanning Gel | 120 ml

An easy-to-spread tanning gel with fine golden shimmer particles that reflect light and highlight the appearance of a tan.

How to Use+

Apply Red Cherry evenly to clean, dry skin for a warm, reddish-toned tanning look, or use Deep Tan Oil for a golden-bronze appearance during sun exposure.

The two products may be incorporated into the same routine according to their individual instructions. Apply Glittery Gold Gel as a final step for a soft golden shimmer.

Reapply as needed and according to each product’s label.

Highlighted Ingredients+

Red Cherry: Coconut oil, olive oil, carrot oil, argan oil, grapeseed oil, beeswax.

Deep Tan: Carrot oil, henna oil, coconut oil, olive oil, shea butter, cocoa butter, vitamin E.

Glittery Gold: Macadamia oil, argan oil, jojoba oil, grapeseed oil, sunflower oil, coconut oil, paraffin oil.

Sun Safety+

These products do not replace sunscreen. Use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher and avoid prolonged sun exposure.
