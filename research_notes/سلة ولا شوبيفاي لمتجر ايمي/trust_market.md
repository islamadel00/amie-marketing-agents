# Saudi shopper trust, market share and conversion effects of Salla, Zid and Shopify (for a Saudi DTC beauty/body-care brand, 2025–2026)

Research method note: done 2026-10-29 with web search only. Direct page fetches to storeleads.app, salla.com and arabnews.pk were blocked by the session network allowlist, so StoreLeads, Salla and Arab News figures below come from search-result extracts of those pages, not full-page reads. Most "Salla vs Zid vs Shopify" content online is agency or vendor marketing. It is flagged as such below.

## 1. Market share, merchant counts and GMV of Salla, Zid and Shopify in KSA; notable beauty brands on each

### Takeaway
No independent, audited 2025–2026 market-share figure exists. Company claims put Salla well ahead of Zid in merchants and GMV: Salla reports about 68k to 80k+ active merchants and US$7B to 13B+ cumulative GMV, while Zid reports about 12k+ merchants and SAR 12B+ cumulative sales. Tracker data (StoreLeads 2026) shows roughly 9.3k live Salla stores and about 14.4k live Zid stores in KSA, plus about 2.4k+ live Shopify stores across the KSA regions that appeared in search results. Shopify is growing fast from a small base. Different sources measure different things, so they contradict each other.

### Cited Findings
**Salla (company claims, not audited)**
- Salla says it has more than 68,000 active merchants, reaches over 15M consumers and has processed more than US$13.3B in lifetime GMV (50B+ SAR). It also says it has handled up to 15M transactions in a single day. G2 and Crunchbase use the same wording, which suggests a company-supplied blurb rather than independent verification. Undated, probably 2025. — [Crunchbase](https://crunchbase.com/organization/salla); [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/salla/reviews)
- In March 2024, Salla raised US$130M in a pre-IPO round led by Investcorp and Sanabil (PIF). At the time it claimed "over 80 thousand active merchants" and "over $7 billion in GMV since 2020". — [PostMoney](https://postmoney.net/salla-130-million/); [EntArabi](https://entarabi.com/en/2024/03/saudi-ecommerce-enablement-platform-salla-raises-130-million-in-pre-ipo-round/)

This may reflect a change from "registered" to "active" merchants, but that cannot be verified. - Isnaad, a fulfilment provider, says it serves "a number of Saudi Arabia's leading beauty and personal-care brands" across Salla, Zid and Shopify, but names none. — [Isnaad beauty](https://www.isnaad.ai/en/verticals-beauty)

- In KSA, Salla is the volume leader by merchants and GMV, and Zid is a clear second. Shopify's KSA footprint is small in absolute terms, probably low thousands of live stores, but growing quickly, possibly because of agency push and cross-border or GCC brands.
- For a beauty/body-care DTC brand, Zid has a visible local beauty cluster, so a'mie being on Zid is in line with category peers.

## 2. Do Saudi shoppers recognise or trust Salla/Zid checkouts, and does Shopify's checkout affect trust?

### Takeaway
No consumer survey, academic study or official statement was found that measures Saudi shopper trust in Salla or Zid checkouts versus Shopify checkout. The evidence is indirect. Salla has consumer-facing touchpoints: the Mahally marketplace app with a single multi-store cart, a store-credit wallet and a one-page checkout. Shopify's signature trust and speed asset, Shop Pay, is unavailable to Saudi-registered stores, because it requires Shopify Payments, which does not support KSA entities.

### Cited Findings
- I found no named consumer survey (CST, Monsha'at, MoC or a research firm) on trust in local platforms versus Shopify checkout. — [search finding; e.g. comparison blogs ijjad](https://www.ijjad.com/salla-vs-zid-vs-shopify-saudi-retailers)
- A vendor-attributed claim, repeated on a blog, says "one in two Saudi online shoppers has purchased from a Salla-powered store". It is unverified. — [task.com.sa blog](https://task.com.sa/en/blog/saudi-ecommerce-platforms-salla-zid-2016-2025)
- Mahally (محلي) is Salla's consumer app. Products from different Salla stores can go into a single cart with one checkout. The app wallet is store credit earned by sharing links, not a payment wallet. Latest app update: 25 October 2026. — [Google Play Mahally](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=sa.salla.mahly&hl=en); [App Store Mahally](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%84%D9%8A-mahally/id6443902847)
- Salla's official info page says the consumer interface offers a one-page checkout and support for loyalty points, e-wallets and gift cards. — [Salla AI-info page](https://salla.com/en-sa/ai-info)
- Salla's customer wallet and cashback feature keeps a balance inside the merchant's own store to drive repeat purchases. It is a merchant feature, not a cross-store wallet. — [Salla releases](https://salla.com/en-ae/releases)
- Salla merchants can switch on digital wallets such as stc pay and PayPal. Help pages are dated around August 2026. — [Salla help: digital wallets](https://help.salla.sa/en/article/manage-digital-wallets/k93ekm8hwa183qrplyil2eaa)
- No "Salla Pay"-branded consumer wallet or 2026 unified-checkout announcement was found.
- Shop Pay is built on Shopify Payments. Saudi-registered stores cannot use Shopify Payments, so Shop Pay does not appear at checkout. Saudi merchants instead use third-party gateways (Moyasar, PayTabs, HyperPay, Tap, Checkout.com) for mada and Apple Pay, and Shopify adds a third-party transaction fee. — [weareautumn](https://www.weareautumn.com/blog/shopify-checkout-optimization-saudi-arabia-uae); [cartdna](https://cartdna.com/en/shopify-payment-guide/middle-east/saudi-arabia); [Experts in Shopify (UAE, same dependency)](https://blog.expertsinshopify.com/ecommerce-uae/shopify-pay-vs-shopify-payments-uae.html)
- One 2026 agency guide says Shopify Payments supports mada for UAE-registered merchants only. Saudi entities still need local gateways. — [search summary of Shopify-KSA guides, e.g. oneonic](https://oneonic.com/shopify-for-saudi-arabia/)
- Apple Pay on Shopify in KSA depends on the gateway (PayTabs, Urway and others). This comes from a 2023 user forum thread and is unverified. — [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/does-apple-pay-work-in-saudi-arabia-for-quicker-checkouts/266362/1)
- Agency opinion, not data: make mada the first visible payment option, and localise checkout copy and error messages into Arabic. — [weareautumn](https://www.weareautumn.com/blog/shopify-checkout-optimization-saudi-arabia-uae)

### Inferences
- Shoppers mostly trust the payment brands they see at checkout (mada, Apple Pay, Tabby, Tamara) and the merchant's own brand. There is no evidence that shoppers notice or value the underlying platform name.
- Salla has some platform-level brand exposure through Mahally. Zid has less consumer-facing presence. Shopify's main KSA disadvantage is losing Shop Pay and native wallets, not an "international feel".
- Any trust difference is an assumption until tested on a'mie's own funnel. No published evidence supports a platform-driven trust effect in KSA.

### Gaps
- No survey data on platform recognition. The Salla "1 in 2 shoppers" claim is unsourced.
- No Mahally download, user or GMV numbers were found.
- No data on whether Shopify's checkout layout (RTL quality, the separate checkout page) changes Saudi abandonment.

## 3. Required trust signals in KSA (Business Platform certificate, e-commerce law, VAT, Arabic invoices) and platform support

### Takeaway
Store verification has moved from Maroof to the Saudi Business Center's Business Platform (business.sa). The Ministry of Commerce announced this on 29 March 2023, and Salla's help centre calls it the only approved platform. The e-commerce law requires disclosure of trade name, CR, contact details and (if registered) VAT number. ZATCA e-invoicing (Fatoora) applies to VAT-registered businesses. Salla and Zid build in local compliance, while Shopify relies on apps. That last point is agency-reported.

### Cited Findings
- The MoC announced that e-stores should register in the Business Platform, operated by the Saudi Business Center, instead of Maroof (29 March 2023). — [MoC news](https://mc.gov.sa/en/mediacenter/News/Pages/29-03-23-02.aspx)
- Salla's help centre says the Business Platform is the only approved platform for verifying online stores. Requirements are a valid CR or freelance certificate and a commercial bank account. Steps are Nafath login, applying for e-commerce verification, proving domain ownership with a DNS TXT record, then submitting. — [Salla help: Business Platform](https://help.salla.sa/en/article/verify-store-on-business-platform-instead-of-maroof/gno9ilestioyspm61yu5buzp)
- The domain TXT-record method is platform-agnostic, so it works on Shopify as well. That is an inference from the step list.
- Some 2026 third-party guides still describe Maroof as current, which conflicts with the official source. One undated guide claims Salla and Zid "often require" Maroof. — [middleeastcommerce](https://middleeastcommerce.net/blog/maroof-store-verification); [saudisellerhub](https://saudisellerhub.com/maroof-store-verification-saudi-arabia/)
- The e-commerce law (Royal Decree, 2019) requires a store to show its trade name, contact details and CR number, plus the VAT number if VAT-registered. A privacy policy is expected. — [Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/e-commerce-law-in-saudi-arabia); [Global Compliance News 2020](https://www.globalcompliancenews.com/2020/03/16/saudi-arabia-regulates-e-commerce/)
- VAT: mandatory registration above SAR 375k in annual taxable sales, voluntary from SAR 187.5k, standard rate 15%. Fatoora e-invoicing is mandatory for VAT-registered businesses. — [origami.sa](https://origami.sa/en/blog/saudi-ecommerce-law-2026-maroof-compliance/); [swissmena](https://swissmena.com/2026-rules-online-retail-saudi-arabia/)
- Terms, return policies and delivery details should be available in Arabic. This comes from a consultancy blog, and no official text on an Arabic-invoice rule was found. — [ghazzawi law firm](https://www.ghazzawilawfirm.com/insights/compliance-steps-for-online-businesses/)
- Penalties of SAR 10k to 500k are cited by a single blog and are unverified.
- Platform support, agency-reported: Salla and Zid have mada, STC Pay, Tabby/Tamara and ZATCA Phase 2 built in, while Shopify gets these through apps. — [isnaad comparison](https://www.isnaad.ai/en/salla-vs-zid-vs-shopify-comparison)

### Inferences
- The legally required trust signals (CR, VAT number, Business Platform badge, Arabic policies) can be shown on any of the three platforms. Salla and Zid lower the setup effort, and Shopify needs a local gateway, a ZATCA app and an Arabic theme setup.

### Gaps
- Not verified whether Zid has a dashboard integration for the Business Platform certificate badge.
- No official confirmation of which Shopify ZATCA apps are Phase 2 (integration-phase) certified.

## 4. Evidence of conversion-rate changes when Saudi brands migrated between Salla/Zid and Shopify

### Takeaway
No published before/after conversion-rate case study was found in either direction for a named Saudi brand. What exists is migration-agency marketing about effort, cost and SEO risk. The only tracker data on direction, StoreLeads, shows Salla as the top destination for Zid churners, not Shopify.

### Cited Findings
- I found no named Saudi beauty brand case study with conversion data for a Salla/Zid to Shopify (Plus) move. — [search results incl. weareautumn](https://www.weareautumn.com/blog/salla-to-shopify-plus-migration-gcc)
- Agency claims: 4 to 8 weeks for a store with 1,000 to 2,000 SKUs, costing SAR 30k to 200k. Brands move for multi-market, multi-currency and multi-language needs. One agency says Salla does not natively support multi-currency or market pricing (unverified). — [Codilar](https://www.codilar.sa/blog/salla-to-shopify-migration-the-saudi-merchants-guide-in-2026); [Magneto IT](https://magnetoitsolutions.com/sa/blog/migrate-salla-zid-shopify)
- SEO risk: skipping 301 redirects reportedly costs "3 to 6 months of search equity" (agency claim). — [search summary, e.g. innovatrixinfotech](https://www.innovatrixinfotech.com/blog/salla-vs-shopify-saudi-arabia-2026)
- StoreLeads (2026, 90 days): Zid lost 65 merchants to other platforms, mostly to Salla. Salla lost 106. — [StoreLeads Zid](https://storeleads.app/reports/zid)

### Inferences
- Since no evidence shows a conversion uplift from switching, a migration's case must rest on features (GCC expansion, theme and app ecosystem) and be weighed against migration cost and SEO risk. a'mie's CLAUDE.md also notes that URLs change on rename.

### Gaps
- No audited A/B or before/after data. Agency case studies are all unnamed or promotional.

## 5. Consumer payment and device preferences in KSA (BNPL, Apple Pay/mada, mobile) as they relate to platform choice

### Takeaway
mada is essential, Apple Pay is the leading wallet, and Tabby and Tamara dominate BNPL, which is heavily used in fashion and beauty. Commerce is mobile-first. Salla and Zid support all of these natively. On Shopify they depend on third-party gateways and apps, and Shop Pay is not available. Exact shares vary widely by source.

### Cited Findings
- mada card e-commerce sales grew 47.3% from January to October 2025, adding about SAR 9.9B year on year. The figure covers mada only and excludes credit cards. — [Okaz / Arabic press via search](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2180878)
- SAMA reportedly said mada e-commerce transaction value rose 71% year on year in Q3 2025. This is a secondary citation in agency guides. — [Shopify-KSA guides search summary, e.g. cartworks](https://cartworks.io/blogs/shopify/selling-on-shopify-saudi-arabia-payments)
- mada share estimates conflict: about 22% of consumer preference, about 40% of payments, or about 93% of card payments, depending on the metric. — [PaymentBrief](https://paymentbrief.com/markets/saudi-arabia/); [apaya](https://www.apaya.io/post/payment-methods-mena-merchants-2026-uae-ksa-digital-wallets); [innovatrixinfotech](https://www.innovatrixinfotech.com/blog/saudi-arabia-payment-methods-ecommerce-integration-2026)
- Apple Pay: about 36% share of preferred online payment in one survey, and more than 20% of online wallet checkouts in 2025 in another. Sources are agency or payments blogs. — [kae.com](https://www.kae.com/featured-insights/shifting-payment-behaviours-in-the-middle-east); [PaymentBrief](https://paymentbrief.com/markets/saudi-arabia/)
- BNPL: Redseer says BNPL was more than 20% of KSA online retail transaction value in 2024, with Tabby and Tamara holding more than 95% of BNPL volume. — [Redseer](https://redseer.com/articles/bnpl-a-key-growth-engine-for-online-retail-in-ksa/)
- Another estimate puts Tabby and Tamara at about 93% of Saudi BNPL, with about $13.2B combined GMV in 2025 out of a roughly $14.2B market. This is a secondary site. — [saudimergersacquisitions](https://saudimergersacquisitions.com/insights/article/tamara-vs-tabby-whats-next-in-saudi-bnpl-consolidation)
- In fashion and beauty, BNPL reportedly accounts for 25 to 30% of checkout completions. This is an agency claim. — [innovatrixinfotech / payments guides search summary](https://www.innovatrixinfotech.com/blog/saudi-arabia-payment-methods-ecommerce-integration-2026)
- Women reportedly make up about 85% of Saudi BNPL users (2025). This is unverified. — [PaymentBrief](https://paymentbrief.com/markets/saudi-arabia/)
- Tabby was valued at more than $4.5B after a secondary share sale. — [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/46823/saudi-bnpl-startup-tabby-increases-valuation-to-45bn)
- Mobile share of commerce is about 75%, and cash on delivery is about 10% of payments or under 30% in urban markets. These come from blogs. — [webmedic](https://webmedic.com/ecommerce-in-saudi-arabia); [PaymentBrief](https://paymentbrief.com/markets/saudi-arabia/)
- Mordor Intelligence lists "low BNPL trust among older shoppers" as a growth restraint. This is a paid-report summary. — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-ecommerce-market)

### Inferences
- For a female-skewed beauty/body-care brand, prominent Tabby/Tamara, Apple Pay and mada options on a fast mobile checkout probably matter more for conversion than which platform runs the store. Every platform can offer them. On Shopify they cost extra integration work and fees.

### Gaps
- No SAMA primary breakdown of e-commerce checkouts by payment method. No beauty-specific payment mix from a primary source.
